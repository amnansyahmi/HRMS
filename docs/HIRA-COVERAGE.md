# HIRA feature coverage

Comparison reviewed on 8 October 2026 against Kuasa's public product pages. This is an independently implemented HR workspace; public descriptions cannot establish exact parity with every private HIRA screen, integration or calculation. The repository includes the workflows below, with the stated deployment requirements and limits.

| HIRA area               | Implemented in Nonymauz People                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Configuration or remaining limits                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| HRIS                    | Employee profiles, Malaysian identity/payroll fields, dependants, education and employment history, departments, reusable designations, configurable employee status/type labels with safe access behaviour, employee files, expiry reminders, equipment, dated job changes, onboarding/offboarding checklists, public onboarding submissions with required documents and HR approval, invitations, CSV import, letters, announcements and read receipts. | Configure SMTP and scheduled maintenance for delivery/reminders. Letters print through the browser. Import is limited to 100 employees per batch.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Attendance and leave    | Clock-in/out, GPS workplace boundaries, person/department shifts, rotating schedules and overnight shifts, configurable pre-shift reminders, corrections, lateness explanations, annual/sick/custom leave, hourly/half-day requests, carry-forward/accrual, overtime, time-off and manager/HR approval.                                                                                                                                                   | HR enters applicable national/state holidays and leave terms. Geolocation needs phone permission; location readings are not proof of physical presence. Fixed conventional half-day intervals require review against unusual shifts.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Payroll                 | Monthly, weekly, fortnightly, off-cycle and final-settlement runs; person/department/company drafts, separate payroll view/prepare/approve/payment grants, allowances, bonus/commission, dated salary proration, approved unpaid leave/overtime/claims, contribution schedules, 2026 PCB, month-end reconciliation across published runs, refreshed draft inputs, review and immutable publication, employee payslips, payment vouchers and exports.      | Interim short-cycle runs require verified manual contributions; the final pay run reconciles monthly totals and subtracts earlier deductions. Independently validate real payroll. No LHDN approval, statutory submission or bank transfer integration. EA/CP22/CP22A exports are preparation worksheets. Unsupported profiles/years require verified manual amounts; rest-day ordinary pay and lateness adjustments require HR review. |
| Claims                  | Configurable types, annual/monthly/per-request limits, department eligibility, mileage, private receipts, OCR suggestions, staged approval, return with a correction reason, same-record resubmission and immutable submission history, balances/reports and reimbursement with salary or separate vouchers.                                                                                                                                              | OCR needs Tesseract or the optional worker. Suggestions require review. Bank exports are generic CSV, not bank-specific payment files.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Recruit                 | Public jobs/applications, screening questions, private parsed resumes, AI resume review, copied jobs, stages/interviews, assessment invitations, hiring to employee/onboarding, retained resume and assessment history.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | AI needs a configured ai-nonymauz-cloud service. PDF/DOCX parsing may fail on scanned or unusual documents. No external job-board synchronization.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Assessments             | Candidate/employee skills tests, original DISC/DOPE reflection questionnaires, team profiles, reusable weighted evaluation templates/cycles, final evaluations and historical comparisons.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Reflection questionnaires are original aids, not proprietary HIRA scoring or validated psychometric instruments.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Goals and performance   | Company/team/person goals, related goals, progress updates, four scorecard perspectives and evaluation histories.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | HR selects appropriate measures and review policies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Ask Lin / People AI     | Seven HR specialists (leave, claims/OT, attendance, recruitment, performance, payroll and letters), plus resume, meeting and preference helpers; sourced context, private chat history, curated Employment Act references and confirmed request/stage/letter/clock actions and owner-only reminder configuration.                                                                                                                                         | Provider credentials, opt-in and per-specialist read/tool controls required. Configuration cards currently change the pre-shift reminder lead time; broader settings remain in the owner form. Legal references are a limited curated set. AI responses need human review; actions recheck permissions and never execute automatically.                                                                                                 |
| Meeting Notes           | Browser recording, private audio upload/playback, BM/English transcription adapter, timestamped segments/flags, editable speakers and talk time, reviewed summaries/actions, selected-person sharing, employee links and ICS import.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | HIRA's public page labels Meeting Notes as coming soon. Our worker must be deployed and tested with its models. Optional diarization needs accepted model terms/token. Worker queue is in-process; timed-out jobs need retry. ICS imports explicit occurrences; no live calendar sync.                                                                                                                                                  |
| Phone experience        | Responsive navigation/forms/tables, home-screen manifest/icons, installation prompt, safe areas and fixed-scale viewport/gesture controls.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | OS accessibility settings may override zoom restrictions. Offline mode is a public reconnect screen; private HR data is not cached. Recording needs HTTPS and the page to remain open.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Accounts and operations | Temporary isolated demo login, real accounts, roles/tenant isolation, verification/reset, encrypted TOTP/recovery codes, email outbox, notifications, authenticated maintenance endpoint, database health and encrypted backup/empty-target restore.                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Production PostgreSQL, canonical URL, SMTP, encryption keys, cron scheduling, monitoring and backup storage are deployment responsibilities. No live provider delivery has been certified by local tests.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
